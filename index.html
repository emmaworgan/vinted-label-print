Parcel label sheet

Shrink Vinted shipping labels to fit label paper with four labels per A4 sheet, then print them from any printer.

1Add your labels

Drop label PDFs here or tap to choose

PDF, PNG or JPG. Add several at once.

    Each label is trimmed to its printed area automatically. If the drop-off instructions got included, use Adjust to draw a box around just the label.

    2Match your label paper

    Check the packet: most 4-per-sheet paper is 99.1 × 139 mm. Choose Custom and enter the sizes from the packet if yours is different.

    Sizes and alignment

    If the print lands slightly off the labels, use the nudge boxes. Negative numbers move it left or up. These settings are remembered on this device.

    3Choose where to print

    Tap a spot to switch it between Print here and Used. Labels fill the free spots in order, so a half-used sheet can go back in the printer.

    4Download and print

    1. Open the PDF and print it. In the print settings, set scale to 100% or Actual size and turn off Fit to page, or the labels won't line up.
    2. First time? Print on plain paper and hold it against a label sheet up to the light to check alignment.
    Your sheet preview appears here once you add a label.

    Your labels stay on your device. Files are read and rearranged inside your browser and aren't uploaded anywhere.

    Adjust label area

    Drag a box around the part to print. Everything outside it is left off.
